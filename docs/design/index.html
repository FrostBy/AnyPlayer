Interactive design preview. Artwork, signal, metadata and playback are illustrative.IDEA-native components in the final plugin.

Content gets the space

Video and images occupy the editor. Audio gives that space to the spectrogram. Transport stays in one predictable region.

Analysis stays close

Open the analysis drawer below video or inspect the track at full size. Both views share the player's source timeline.

Details when needed

Tracks, subtitles and speed live in compact actions. MediaInfo has its own readable view with a complete report.

Reference board

Three sources, each with a specific job. The final visual system comes from the active IDEA theme.

UI uses theme roles

Panel, editor, text, separator, selection and focus colors come from IDEA in production. Dark/light values here approximate the standard themes for review.

EditorPanelFocusText

Signal has its own scale

A stable dark-blue, violet, orange and white intensity ramp keeps the plot meaningful across themes. Hz, source time and dB remain readable at either brightness.
